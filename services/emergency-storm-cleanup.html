Emergency Storm Cleanup in Winnipeg — Available 24/7

A tree down on your garage or blocking your driveway isn't something that can wait for a scheduled appointment. Our matched crews handle emergency calls around the clock, prioritizing anything that's actively dangerous or blocking access to your home.

What to do right after storm damage

  • If any part of the tree is touching or near a power line, stay well back and call Manitoba Hydro immediately — don't approach it.
  • Don't try to cut or move large limbs yourself, especially if the tree is still under tension from a partial fall.
  • Keep kids and pets away from any leaning or split tree until it's been assessed.
  • Photograph the damage before cleanup starts — it'll help if you're filing an insurance claim.

How our emergency response works

  1. Fast dispatch. Emergency requests are flagged and prioritized so a local crew can reach you quickly, day or night.
  2. Hazard stabilization. If a tree is only partially down, the crew's first job is making the site safe before full removal.
  3. Removal & cleanup. The tree is taken down, debris hauled away, and the site left safe and clean.
