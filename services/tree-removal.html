Tree Removal

Tree Removal in Winnipeg, Done Safely and Right

Whether it's a dead elm leaning toward the garage or a healthy tree that's simply in the wrong spot, full tree removal isn't a job for a weekend and a chainsaw. Our matched crews are licensed, insured, and equipped to take down trees of any size — including the tricky ones wedged between a fence, a shed, and a power line.

When you need full removal

  • The tree is dead, dying, or visibly diseased
  • Storm damage has left it cracked, split, or leaning
  • Roots are threatening a foundation, driveway, or sewer line
  • It's blocking a construction, landscaping, or fencing project
  • It's simply grown too large or too close to the house

How our local crews handle a removal

Every job starts with a free, no-obligation assessment — a crew looks at the tree's size, condition, and access before quoting a price. From there:

  1. Site check. Confirming property lines, boulevard status, and anything overhead (power lines, structures) that changes the approach.
  2. Safe takedown. Larger, open-yard trees usually come down as a whole section; tight-access trees are taken down piece by piece with rigging to protect nearby structures.
  3. Full cleanup. Wood and brush are hauled away and the site is left clean — stump grinding can be added on if you want the stump gone too.
Boulevard trees belong to the City of Winnipeg — homeowners can't remove or prune them without City approval. And if the tree is a Dutch Elm Disease–infected American elm, it needs to be reported to 311 before it comes down, with specific rules for handling the wood.

What it costs
