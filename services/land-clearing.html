Land & Lot Clearing in Winnipeg

Whether you're prepping a lot for new construction, clearing an overgrown acreage, or starting a landscaping project from scratch, lot clearing takes more than a single tree removal — it needs a crew that can handle trees, brush, and stumps across the whole property in one pass.

What's included

  • Removal of all unwanted trees, regardless of size or number
  • Clearing of brush, deadfall, and undergrowth
  • Stump grinding so the site is ready to grade or build on
  • Full debris hauling, leaving a clean, construction- or landscaping-ready site

Before you clear

A few things are worth checking before work starts: confirming property lines so you're not clearing anything on a neighbour's land, locating underground utilities, and identifying any American elms on the lot, which fall under Manitoba's Dutch Elm Disease rules even on land slated for clearing. Larger lots or acreages may also have municipal permitting requirements depending on scope.

If there are elms on the property, they still need to be checked for Dutch Elm Disease and, if infected, reported to 311 before removal — even on a lot you're clearing for construction.

Our process

  1. Site walk. A crew walks the property to scope tree count, size, and access for equipment.
  2. Clearing. Trees, brush, and undergrowth are removed methodically across the site.
  3. Grinding & grading prep. Stumps are ground out and the site is left clear and debris-free.
