How Much Does Tree Removal Cost in Winnipeg? (2026 Price Guide)

If you've never had a tree removed before, the price range you'll see online — anywhere from $150 to $3,000+ — probably isn't very helpful. The truth is that tree removal cost in Winnipeg comes down to a handful of specific factors, and once you know what they are, you can get a pretty accurate estimate before a crew ever shows up.

Average tree removal costs in Winnipeg

These are typical ranges homeowners see for a straightforward residential removal, including cleanup and hauling away the wood:

Tree sizeTypical cost
Small (under 30 ft)$150 – $450
Medium (30–60 ft)$450 – $1,000
Large (60–80+ ft)$1,000 – $2,500+
Stump grinding (per stump)$100 – $450

Emergency or storm-related removals — think a tree down on your garage after a summer windstorm — typically run 25–50% higher than a scheduled removal, mostly because of after-hours labour and the added risk of working around damaged structures or power lines.

What actually drives the price up

Size and height

This is the biggest factor. A bigger tree means more time, more equipment, and more wood to haul away.

Access

A tree in an open front yard is cheap to remove. A tree wedged in a narrow backyard, close to a fence, shed, or power line, takes longer and often needs to be taken down in sections by hand — which adds cost.

Condition

Dead, diseased, or storm-damaged trees can be more dangerous to remove than healthy ones, which sometimes increases labour cost. If the tree is a Dutch Elm Disease–infected elm, there are also specific disposal rules to follow — see our guide to Dutch Elm Disease in Winnipeg for what that involves.

Stump removal

Most quotes treat stump grinding as a separate line item. If you plan to landscape or plant over the spot, it's worth budgeting for.

Winnipeg-specific note: trees on city boulevards can't be removed by homeowners — that requires the City of Winnipeg. And if the tree in question is an American elm, pruning is restricted April 1–July 31 under Manitoba's Dutch Elm Disease control rules. Always confirm current requirements with 311 before doing anything to a boulevard or elm tree.

How to get an accurate quote

The only way to get a real number is to have someone assess the tree — its size, location, condition, and how hard it'll be to access. The good news is that doesn't need to cost you anything or take much of your time.
